### 👨‍💻 About Me
- 💼 **Day job:** Store Manager at **SmartKoshk** (El-Nahhas branch, Cairo) since Oct 2023. 50M+ EGP in branch sales at 140% of quota, 190% in two consecutive periods, top sales three months running.
- 🚀 **Freelance:** Independent App Developer since Oct 2026, remote, 30+ hrs/week. Four apps shipped end to end, from interface and database to permissions and tests.
- 🔭 **Building:** **Mudev ERP** (cloud ERP for startups, Arabic/English RTL/LTR) and **SKVault** (Arabic-first PWA for a five-branch retailer, Next.js + Supabase, RLS, realtime updates, settlement queue). Live demo: [skvault-demo.vercel.app](https://skvault-demo.vercel.app)
- 📱 **Android:** **DotPray** (offline prayer clock in Java, three resizable widgets, Qibla compass, Arabic/English RTL, 980+ automated checks) and **Shizuku Shot** (no-root screenshots of protected screens, Kotlin + Jetpack Compose).
- 🛍️ **SmartKoshk app:** native Shopify storefront in Kotlin + Jetpack Compose.
- 🎓 **Education:** Bachelor's in **Management Information Systems (MIS)**, HICMIS, 2022.
- 💡 **Approach:** Bridging the gap between business operations and software by turning complex workflows into clean, automated systems.
- ⚡ **Off the Clock:** Dominating the top/mid lanes in *Wild Rift* or unwinding at my favorite local café after a morning shift.
